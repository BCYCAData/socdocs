The Strengthen Our Community project brings together residents, coordinators, and partner organizations to improve local preparedness through shared information and practical tools.

This page provides a public history overview and a structure for recording key project milestones as they are confirmed.

## Project origin

The project began as a community-focused effort to improve planning readiness, make local information easier to access, and support place-based coordination.

Its delivery model combines digital tools, local engagement, and ongoing collaboration between operational and community teams.
