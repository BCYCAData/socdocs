Community Map provides area-wide spatial context for your local community.
It helps you view boundaries, address points, and community-relevant locations in one place.

## What you can see

- community boundary context
- address and registration-related points
- additional overlays available through layer controls

## Typical map workflow

1. Open Community Map from your community page.
2. Wait for map layers to finish loading.
3. Use layer controls to show or hide overlays.
4. Pan and zoom to inspect local context around your area.

## Reading map layers

- boundary layers define the community extent
- point layers show known location data relevant to community operations
- clustered points may separate as you zoom in

## Troubleshooting

- map unavailable message: your area may not have a valid extent yet
- missing layer details: toggle layers off/on and reload
