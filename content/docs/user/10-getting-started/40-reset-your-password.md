If you cannot sign in, use the password reset flow to set a new password securely.
Reset links are single-use and tied to a recovery session.

## Reset steps

1. Open the password reset request page.
2. Enter your account email and submit.
3. Open the reset email and follow the recovery link.
4. Enter a new password and submit.
5. Sign in with your new password.

## Troubleshooting

- No email received: check spam/junk and retry.
- Expired link: request a fresh reset email.
- Reset page redirects to sign-in: open the newest recovery link and try again.

## Security notes

- Choose a new password you have not used before.
- Keep recovery links private and do not share them.
