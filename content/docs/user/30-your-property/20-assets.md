Assets records important property features that support readiness and planning.
Keeping asset details current improves map quality and decision context.

## Typical asset records

- water sources
- access-related features
- other property resources captured by available templates

## Add or update an asset

1. Open your property.
2. Go to Assets or use My Map with the relevant asset template.
3. Enter required details.
4. Save and confirm the record appears correctly.

## Review guidance

- remove duplicate or outdated entries
- verify map placement if a feature location changed
- keep naming clear so future updates are easier
