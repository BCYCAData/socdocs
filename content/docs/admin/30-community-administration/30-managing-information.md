Managing Information publishes resident-facing announcements and guidance updates.
Use this flow for timely communications that do not belong in events or workshops.

## Authoring workflow

1. Draft the update with a clear title.
2. Verify message scope and audience.
3. Publish and check resident visibility.
4. Revise or retire outdated notices.

## Content tips

- keep messages concise and action-oriented
- include dates when timing matters
- avoid duplicating event-specific details better suited to Events
