SOC is designed to keep resident data protected while enabling planning and preparedness features.
Privacy settings and role-based access controls determine how data is used in the platform.

## What data is used

- profile details you provide in account and onboarding forms
- property-linked information you record in SOC
- map features you create for planning context

## How access is controlled

- users can access their own resident/profile context
- elevated roles have scoped access based on assignment and permission rules
- protected routes require authentication

## Your responsibilities

- keep contact and profile data accurate
- sign out on shared devices
- report suspicious account activity promptly

## Privacy best practices

- avoid sharing login credentials
- update password if you receive unexpected account notices
- review profile data periodically and remove stale details where possible
