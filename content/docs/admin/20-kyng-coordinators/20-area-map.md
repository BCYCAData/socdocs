Area Map gives coordinators spatial visibility across their assigned KYNG area.
It supports quick review of area context, address points, and property-related layers.

## What to check on the map

- area boundary and visible coverage
- property and address-point context
- optional layers enabled for specific review tasks

## Typical workflow

1. Open the map from the coordinator dashboard.
2. Confirm you are in the correct KYNG area.
3. Toggle overlays based on the current task.
4. Inspect locations requiring follow-up.

## Practical tips

- use layer controls to reduce clutter during review
- verify zoom level before interpreting clustered points
- cross-check map findings with unregistered-address and resident-admin pages
