Information is the primary feed for community announcements, guidance, and local updates.
Check this page to stay current on changes that affect your area.

## What to look for

- new announcements
- planning guidance updates
- notices relevant to your community section

## Using the information feed

1. Open Information from your community navigation.
2. Review newest items first.
3. Open details for posts related to your property or participation plans.
4. Revisit frequently during active community periods.

## Good practices

- verify post dates before acting on older information
- keep key updates in mind when editing your property plans
