Community Administration manages resident-facing content and map-linked community context.
This section covers recurring publishing and maintenance workflows.

## Core responsibilities

- maintain events, information, and workshop content
- keep community map context reliable
- review pending updates and remove stale entries

## Recommended cadence

1. Review community updates regularly.
2. Publish new items with clear titles and dates.
3. Archive or update outdated content.
4. Verify resident-facing pages show expected results.
