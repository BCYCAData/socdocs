The About Me section stores the core personal information used across resident workflows.
Accurate details improve communication and reduce follow-up during support interactions.

## What to update

- name and contact details
- profile information requested by the form
- any required fields highlighted by completion tracking

## Editing workflow

1. Open About Me from your profile area.
2. Update fields as needed.
3. Save changes.
4. Confirm success state and review any validation messages.

## Validation tips

- Complete required fields before save.
- Use realistic, current contact details.
- Resolve inline field errors before leaving the page.
