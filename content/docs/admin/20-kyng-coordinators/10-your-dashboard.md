The coordinator dashboard summarizes the KYNG areas you are assigned to and the tasks requiring follow-up.
It is the starting point for area-level map review and resident support activity.

## Typical coordinator tasks

- open assigned area views
- review unregistered address lists
- support resident administration needs in scoped areas

## Recommended workflow

1. Confirm assigned KYNG area context.
2. Review pending updates or outreach lists.
3. Open area map and resident-admin views as needed.
4. Record actions and return to dashboard for next task.

## Access notes

- coordinators should only see areas in their assignment scope
- if area access is missing, verify coordinator assignment and permission claims
