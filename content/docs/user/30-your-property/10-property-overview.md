The property area organizes address-linked information used for planning and preparedness.
It is the starting point for assets, hazards, resources, and map-based features.

## Core property areas

- property summary and address context
- assets and hazards records
- resources linked to your property
- map-based feature editing and review

## Recommended usage

1. Open your property record.
2. Review current assets and hazards.
3. Update records that changed recently.
4. Use My Map to verify spatial context and feature placement.

## If you have multiple properties

- confirm you are editing the correct property before saving
- repeat updates per property as needed
