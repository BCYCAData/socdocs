Resources collects supporting information and references linked to your property context.
Use this area to keep useful materials accessible for planning and follow-up actions.

## What you may find here

- property-related references
- supporting planning materials
- links or notes used during preparedness workflows

## Recommended usage

1. Open Resources from your property page.
2. Review existing items for relevance.
3. Add or update entries when new materials become available.
4. Remove outdated items that no longer apply.

## Keep resources useful

- use descriptive titles
- include only information you are likely to revisit
- review periodically to prevent stale content
