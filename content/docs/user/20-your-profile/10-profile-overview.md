Your profile is the main workspace for managing personal details, settings, and property-linked information.
Completion and status indicators help you track what remains to be configured.

## What you can manage here

- About Me information
- account settings
- property records and related map tools
- community-facing information relevant to your area

## Typical actions

1. Review incomplete profile sections.
2. Update personal details.
3. Save changes and confirm success messages.
4. Return to pending sections until profile completion is up to date.

## Good practices

- Keep contact and household details current.
- Use save frequently when editing long forms.
- Recheck profile after major account or property updates.
