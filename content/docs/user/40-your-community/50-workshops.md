Workshops lists community learning sessions and collaborative preparedness activities.
Use this page to track opportunities to build practical readiness skills.

## What to expect

- workshop summaries
- timing and participation details
- updates as sessions are scheduled or revised

## How to use this page

1. Open Workshops from your community section.
2. Review upcoming sessions.
3. Check any preparation notes before attending.
4. Revisit after updates are published.

## Participation tips

- align workshop topics with your current property planning needs
- pair workshop updates with map and information pages for full context
