Hazards records help track risks connected to your property and support preparedness planning.
Use this area to keep known risks visible and current.

## What to record

- hazard features relevant to your property area
- updates when conditions or observations change
- notes needed for future review and planning

## Update workflow

1. Open your property hazards area or My Map.
2. Add or edit the relevant hazard feature.
3. Complete required fields.
4. Save and verify the update appears in list/map views.

## Good practices

- review hazards regularly, especially after major weather events
- keep entries specific and avoid duplicate records
- use clear names and descriptions for easier follow-up
