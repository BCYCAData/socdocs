Managing Workshops supports planning, publishing, and maintaining workshop activities for residents.
This flow complements Events by focusing on workshop-specific operational details.

## Workshop workflow

1. Create workshop entry with topic, timing, and context.
2. Publish and verify resident-facing display.
3. Update details when schedules or formats change.
4. Archive outdated workshops after completion.

## Good practices

- keep workshop descriptions outcome-focused
- align workshop timing with event announcements where relevant
- remove stale sessions promptly
