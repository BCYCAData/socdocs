Your Community is where you find shared updates, events, workshops, and map context for your local area.
Use this section to stay informed and connected with community-level activity.

## What is included

- event listings
- information and announcements
- community map views
- workshop updates and participation details

## Recommended first steps

1. Open the community section linked to your area.
2. Review current information updates.
3. Check upcoming events and workshops.
4. Open the community map for local context.

## When to return

- after major weather events or seasonal risk periods
- when new community activities are announced
- when you need local context before updating property plans
