Project components work together to support local preparedness through community participation, digital tools, and practical delivery activities.

This overview explains how each component contributes to the broader program outcomes.

## Component groups

- community hubs
- KYNG coordination model
- digital mapping capability
- workshops and engagement activities

## How components connect

- community hubs provide local participation and delivery context
- KYNG structures support area-based coordination
- digital mapping supports shared visibility and planning
- workshops translate tools and planning into practical action

## Public communication guidance

When describing components, focus on:

- purpose
- beneficiaries
- observable outcomes

Avoid unverified quantitative claims until source data is approved for publication.
