KYNGs provide the project’s area-based coordination structure, supporting local engagement and operational context.
They help connect residents, coordinators, and admin workflows to place-specific needs.

## Role in the project

- organize local area coordination
- support community communication pathways
- provide structure for map and operational scope boundaries

## Participation model

Document how KYNG participation is structured, including:

- area assignment approach
- coordinator responsibilities
- interaction with residents and admin teams

## Governance notes

- define clear scope boundaries
- align KYNG operations with approved role and permission models
- maintain transparent change management for boundary or assignment updates
