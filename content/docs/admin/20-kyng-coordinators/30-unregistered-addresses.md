Unregistered Addresses helps coordinators identify properties in their area that are not yet linked to resident accounts.
This supports targeted outreach and onboarding assistance.

## What this page provides

- a scoped list of unregistered addresses for your area
- status context for outreach planning
- a working queue for follow-up actions

## Recommended workflow

1. Review the list and sort by priority.
2. Track outreach attempts and follow-up outcomes.
3. Recheck list updates after outreach cycles.

## Good practices

- focus first on high-impact or frequently referenced addresses
- keep notes concise and action-focused
- verify area scope when comparing across KYNG regions
