Use Sign In to access your resident dashboard and protected profile features.
Sign Out ends your active session on the current browser.

## Sign in

1. Open the Sign In page.
2. Enter your registered email and password.
3. Submit and wait for redirect to your profile area.

## Common sign-in errors

- Invalid credentials: check email spelling and password.
- Too many attempts: wait and try again later.
- Session refresh failure: sign in again from the prompt.

## Sign out

1. Open the user menu or sign-out route.
2. Confirm sign-out when prompted.
3. Verify protected pages now redirect to sign-in.

## Security tips

- Sign out on shared devices.
- Do not save passwords in public browsers.
- Reset your password immediately if you suspect account access by someone else.
