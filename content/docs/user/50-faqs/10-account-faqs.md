## How do I create an account?

Start on the Create Account page, validate your address, then enter email and password.
After that, confirm your email to complete activation.

## Why was my address rejected during signup?

This usually means the address was not found, is outside eligible service areas, or there was a temporary validation issue.
Check spelling and suburb first, then try again.

## I did not receive the confirmation email. What should I do?

Check spam or junk folders and confirm you used the correct email.
If needed, repeat the signup flow to request a new confirmation email.

## I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

Use the password reset page, request a recovery email, and follow the latest reset link.
Reset links are single-use and may expire.

## I can sign in but I cannot access a page I expected.

Some pages require specific account state or permissions.
Sign out and sign in again first, then check you are using the correct account.

## How do I change my email or password?

Open Settings in your profile area and start the relevant account flow.
Email changes may require confirmation before the new address is active.

## Why was I signed out automatically?

Sessions can end due to timeout, expiry, or refresh issues.
Sign in again and continue where you left off.
