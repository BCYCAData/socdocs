Spatial Data administration manages templates, layers, and geometry-backed datasets used across SOC maps.
This area controls map reliability for resident, coordinator, and admin workflows.

## Responsibilities

- maintain spatial feature templates and fields
- verify map layer quality and visibility
- review refresh and provenance outcomes where relevant

## Operational workflow

1. Review current layer/template configuration.
2. Apply required updates.
3. Validate map rendering on impacted routes.
4. Confirm no regressions in capture or reporting flows.

## Quality checks

- required template fields are present
- geometry and styling behavior remains consistent
- map pages load without missing layer data
