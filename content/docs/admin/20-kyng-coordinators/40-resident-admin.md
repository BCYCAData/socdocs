Resident Admin allows coordinators to assist residents with scoped account and profile support needs.
Use this area for operational support within your assigned KYNG context.

## Common support actions

- confirm resident record visibility in your area
- assist with onboarding and profile-completion follow-up
- identify when issues require escalation to admin teams

## Safeguards

- act only within assigned area scope
- avoid direct credential handling
- escalate permission or role issues to administrators

## Escalation guidance

- role/permission mismatch
- data issues requiring site-level correction
- unresolved account-access problems after standard checks
