Settings controls account-level options such as sign-in details and personal account preferences.
Use this area when you need to update how your account is secured or configured.

## Common settings tasks

- review account details
- start email change or password change flows
- confirm current account state after profile updates

## Updating account access settings

1. Open Settings from your profile navigation.
2. Choose the setting you want to update.
3. Follow the prompted flow (for example email change or password update).
4. Confirm success messages and, if required, sign in again.

## Tips

- Keep account details current so support and recovery flows work smoothly.
- Use unique passwords and update them if you suspect compromise.
