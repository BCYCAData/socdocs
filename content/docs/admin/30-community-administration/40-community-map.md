Community Map administration controls map layers and visibility that residents use for local context.
Use this area to verify map quality and alignment with current community data.

## What to validate

- expected layers are available and readable
- boundary and point context are accurate
- map performance remains usable at common zoom levels

## Operational workflow

1. Open the community map admin view.
2. Toggle layers relevant to current updates.
3. Confirm data visibility and presentation.
4. Recheck after publishing related content changes.
