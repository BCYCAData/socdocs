The project team includes community participants, operational contributors, and delivery partners.
This page should provide a clear public view of who contributes and what each group is responsible for.

## Team structure

Document contributors by function rather than informal labels, for example:

- project leadership and governance
- community coordination and engagement
- platform and technical delivery
- operations, communications, and support

## Responsibility model

For each team group, capture:

- primary responsibilities
- decision or approval boundaries
- key collaboration interfaces with other groups

## Publication guidance

- include only names and roles approved for public listing
- keep role descriptions current when responsibilities change
- avoid publishing personal contact details unless explicitly approved

## Editorial note

Once owner-approved team details are added, update this page from draft to complete.
