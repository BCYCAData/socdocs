Workshops are the project’s hands-on engagement component, helping participants translate information into practical readiness actions.
They connect community participation with program goals and tool adoption.

## Workshop role

- support practical learning and collaboration
- reinforce key project messages and planning workflows
- create feedback loops for ongoing improvement

## Typical workshop structure

- topic-focused sessions
- guided discussion and practical examples
- follow-up actions linked to community or property planning

## Outcome framing

Use approved evidence to describe:

- participation and engagement quality
- practical capability gains
- contribution to wider project outcomes
